## User Story

> **As** a lead with an active improvement plan, **I want** to register my monthly financial progress and update my situation, **in order to** know whether I am advancing correctly toward my real estate objective.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### E1 — Monthly progress registration

**Given** the user has an active plan,  
**When** they register debts paid or saved amounts,  
**Then** the system must update their monthly progress and show whether they are ahead, on schedule, or behind relative to their plan.

---

### E2 — Eligibility projection

**Given** the user updates their financial progress,  
**When** the system recalculates their situation,  
**Then** it must show an estimated eligibility date or an estimated approach date to the objective.

---

### E3 — Improvement-plan status updates

**Given** the user has an active improvement plan,  
**When** they register progress, complete milestones, or miss planned activities,  
**Then** the system must update the plan status on the platform, using states such as "Not started", "In progress", "Completed", or "Requires adjustment".

---

### E4 — Score recalculation from milestone completion

**Given** the user registers the completion of a financial milestone, such as paying a debt or increasing savings,  
**When** the system validates that progress,  
**Then** it must recalculate the financial score and update the user's classification if applicable.

---

### E5 — Milestone entry and validation

**Given** the user wants to register progress within their improvement plan,  
**When** they enter a financial milestone such as a paid debt, accumulated savings, or improved employment continuity,  
**Then** the system must validate that the entered value is consistent, positive, and does not contradict previously registered financial information.
