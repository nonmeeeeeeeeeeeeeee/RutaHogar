# HU 2 — Lead Prioritization

Provides the sales executive with a prioritized, AI-assisted view of pre-qualified leads. Instead of working through a raw list of contacts, the executive sees who to call first, why, and what action to take — without needing a CRM integration at this stage.

## User Story

> **As** a real estate sales executive, **I want** to view a portfolio of pre-qualified and prioritized leads with AI support, **in order to** focus my time on prospects with the highest probability of advancing in the purchase process and quickly understand the financial context of each lead.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### E1 — Prioritization of high-score leads

**Given** the system has processed the financial scoring of multiple prospects,  
**When** the sales executive accesses the main panel,  
**Then** leads with an "Alto" (High) classification must automatically appear at the top of the list.

---

### E2 — Visual indicators and intelligent explanation

**Given** a lead has an assigned score,  
**When** the executive selects a specific lead,  
**Then** the system must show a visual summary of the indicators (financial burden, stability, etc.) and the intelligent explanation generated to justify that score.

---

### E3 — Filter by priority level

**Given** the executive needs to organize their day,  
**When** they use the filter tool,  
**Then** the system must allow segmenting the view by Alto, Medio, or Bajo categories immediately.

---

### E4 — Suggested commercial action

**Given** the executive reviews the profile of a prioritized lead,  
**When** they view the scoring detail,  
**Then** the system must display a suggested-action label (e.g. "Contact immediately", "Keep in follow-up", or "Request more information") based on the lead's level of readiness.

---

## Notes

- This story deliberately avoids CRM integration (that is [[HU12-CommercialDerivation\|HU 12]]). The dashboard is self-contained.
- The AI-assisted summary in E2 is generated by the same explanation layer as [[HU3-HybridScoring\|HU 3]] — no separate model needed.
- The executive role is enforced at the routing level: only users with role `sales` can access the leads dashboard. See `DashboardLeads.jsx`.
