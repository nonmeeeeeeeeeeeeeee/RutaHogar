# HU 4 — Personalized Improvement Plan Generator

For leads that don't yet qualify, the system generates a personalized, step-by-step action plan based on their specific financial profile — including targeted debt-payment and down-payment savings goals. The plan turns a "not ready" result into a structured path toward purchase readiness.

## User Story

> **As** a lead in the preparation stage, **I want** to receive a personalized improvement plan with recommendations and debt/savings goals, **in order to** know what actions I must take to get closer to my real estate objective.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### E1 — Personalized plan generation

**Given** a user does not meet the conditions to buy their real estate objective,  
**When** they finish their financial assessment,  
**Then** the system must generate a step-by-step improvement plan based on their data.

---

### E2 — Personalized recommendations

**Given** the user has received their score,  
**When** the system identifies improvement opportunities,  
**Then** it must generate recommendations related to savings, debt, employment continuity, timeline, or real estate objective.

---

### E3 — Prioritization by impact

**Given** several recommendations are available,  
**When** the user views their result,  
**Then** the system must order them according to their estimated impact.

---

### E4 — Expected benefit

**Given** the user reviews a recommendation,  
**When** it is shown on screen,  
**Then** it must indicate the expected benefit of applying it.

---

### E5 — Debt-payment plan

**Given** the user has active debts that affect their financial score,  
**When** the system generates the personalized improvement plan,  
**Then** it must create a debt-payment proposal indicating target amount, payment priority, and estimated timeframe to reduce their financial burden.

---

### E6 — Debt-payment ordering

**Given** the user has more than one registered debt,  
**When** they view their improvement plan,  
**Then** the system must order the debts by their impact on the score, outstanding amount, or payment urgency, to guide the user on which to address first.

---

### E7 — Down-payment savings plan

**Given** the user does not have enough savings to cover the required down payment (pie) of their real estate objective,  
**When** the system generates the improvement plan,  
**Then** it must propose a monthly savings goal and an estimated timeframe to reach the required amount.

---

## Notes

- The improvement plan is generated by `generate_improvement_plan()` in `backend/app/scoring.py`. It is currently a deterministic mock based on `risk_codes` — the same interface used by the AI explanation layer.
- Available `risk_codes`: `ingreso_dividendo`, `deuda_alta`, `ahorro_bajo`, `precio_objetivo`, `contrato_independiente`, `continuidad_baja`, `continuidad_media`, `morosidad_alta`, `morosidad_media`.
- The plan is returned as the `improvement_plan` array in the `POST /score` response and stored in `evaluations.recommendations.improvement_plan`.
- Debt/savings goals (E5–E7) are tracked over time in [[HU13-MonthlyPlanTracking\|HU 13]] and map to the `improvement_goals` table. See [[../Database/improvement_goals\|improvement_goals]].
- Users with a High score bypass this flow and are routed to the executive dashboard (see [[HU2-LeadPrioritization\|HU 2]] and [[HU3-HybridScoring\|HU 3]] E6).
- **Numbering note:** documented as `HdU 4` in the E2 informe and as `HU 7` under the E4 plan; the updated sprint plan renumbers it to `HU 4`. Criteria E5–E7 match that document's debt-payment and savings-plan criteria.
