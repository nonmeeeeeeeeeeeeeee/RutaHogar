# HdU 4 — Improvement Plan Generator

For leads that don't yet qualify, the system generates a personalized, step-by-step action plan based on their specific financial profile. The plan updates dynamically as the user re-evaluates over time, turning a "not ready" result into a structured path toward purchase readiness.

## User Story

> **As** a person interested in buying a home, **I want** the system to generate a personalized improvement plan, **in order to** reach the purchase capacity required for the real estate project.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### E1 — Personalized plan

**Given** a user interested in buying a home,  
**When** they submit their data for scoring and do not meet the conditions to purchase the real estate project (i.e. they are not a qualified lead),  
**Then** the system must create a personalized step-by-step plan based on their submitted data to improve their purchasing capacity in the future.

---

### E2 — Dynamic purchase capacity analysis

**Given** an interested user has already submitted their data on the platform,  
**When** they first submit their data or when they update it,  
**Then** the system must perform an updated purchase capacity analysis and update the user's status in the system accordingly.

---

### E3 — Purchase capacity validation and plan necessity check

**Given** a user interested in buying a home,  
**When** they submit the data required for their scoring,  
**Then** the system must validate their purchase capacity and determine whether an improvement plan is necessary or not.

---

### E4 — Orientative recommendations

**Given** the user has received their score,  
**When** the system identifies improvement opportunities,  
**Then** it must display orientative recommendations related to savings, debt reduction, employment continuity, purchase timeline, or real estate objective.

---

## Notes

- The improvement plan is generated by `generate_improvement_plan()` in `backend/app/scoring.py`. It is currently a deterministic mock based on `risk_codes` — the same interface used by the AI explanation layer.
- Available `risk_codes`: `ingreso_dividendo`, `deuda_alta`, `ahorro_bajo`, `precio_objetivo`, `contrato_independiente`, `continuidad_baja`, `continuidad_media`, `morosidad_alta`, `morosidad_media`.
- The plan is returned as the `improvement_plan` array in the `POST /score` response and stored in `evaluations.recommendations.improvement_plan`.
- Users with a High score bypass this flow and are routed to the executive dashboard (see [[HdU2-LeadPrioritization|HdU 2]] and [[HdU3-HybridScoring|HdU 3]] E6).
