# HdU 2 — Lead Prioritization

Provides the sales executive with a prioritized, AI-assisted view of pre-qualified leads. Instead of working through a raw list of contacts, the executive sees who to call first, why, and what action to take — without needing a CRM integration at this stage.

## User Story

> **As** a real estate sales executive, **I want** to view a portfolio of pre-qualified and prioritized leads with AI support, **in order to** focus my time on prospects with the highest probability of advancing in the purchase process and quickly understand the financial context of each lead.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### E1 — Prioritization of high-score leads

**Given** the system has completed the financial scoring of users,  
**When** it identifies leads with an "Alto" (High) classification,  
**Then** those leads must appear at the top of the executive's commercial dashboard.

---

### E2 — Summary with justification for high-score leads

**Given** a user successfully completes their scoring,  
**When** their score is classified as High,  
**Then** the system provides a summary with the financial indicators that justify the score obtained.

---

### E3 — Filter by priority level

**Given** the sales executive accesses the leads panel,  
**When** they select filters by classification or priority,  
**Then** the system must allow differentiated visualization of Alto, Medio, and Bajo profiles.

---

### E4 — Commercial action guidance

**Given** the executive views a prioritized lead,  
**When** they review their profile,  
**Then** the system must suggest a general action — such as "contact soon" or "keep in follow-up" — based on the lead's level of financial readiness.

---

## Notes

- This story deliberately avoids CRM integration (that is [[HdU5-CRMIntegration|HdU 5]]). The dashboard is self-contained.
- The AI-assisted summary in E2 is generated by the same explanation layer as [[HdU3-HybridScoring|HdU 3]] — no separate model needed.
- The executive role is enforced at the routing level: only users with role `sales` can access the leads dashboard. See `DashboardLeads.jsx`.
